# Voice Guide: Patterns & Anti-Patterns

Detailed reference for crafting authentic founder voice content.

## Voice Calibration Deep Dive

When a founder shares example articles, analyze these dimensions:

### Sentence Structure

**Short & Punchy**
> "I built this. It failed. I built it again."

**Long & Flowing**
> "When I first started thinking about the problem of sales data quality, I didn't realize that the issue wasn't the data itself but the fundamental assumption that static databases could keep up with a dynamic world."

**Mixed (Most Common)**
> "I spent six months with Apollo. Here's what happened: 10-15% of contacts were essentially useless—not slightly outdated, but completely stale."

Note their rhythm and mirror it.

### Technical Depth

**Deep Technical**
Uses specific terms, assumes reader knowledge:
> "The agent parsed the DOM, extracted the return policy text using semantic chunking, and matched it against our payment provider taxonomy."

**Accessible Technical**
Explains concepts inline:
> "Our AI agent—think of it like a very thorough research assistant—went to the company's website and read through their terms of service."

**Non-Technical**
Focuses on outcomes:
> "We found out which payment methods they accept without anyone having to manually check."

### Opening Patterns

**The Discovery Moment**
> "I was reviewing agent logs late one night when I found something I didn't program."

**The Counter-Narrative**
> "Everyone says AI will replace salespeople. That's not what I saw in 36 interviews."

**The Specific Problem**
> "10-15% of Apollo contacts don't exist. I tested this across 3 campaigns."

**The Journey Start**
> "I should be upfront: I'm the founder of a competing product. But I also used Apollo for 6 months before building an alternative."

### Closing Patterns

**Invitation to Dialogue**
> "If you've seen similar patterns—or completely different ones—I'd like to hear about it. Find me on LinkedIn."

**The Open Question**
> "The question isn't whether your team will use AI. They already are, probably without telling you. The question is whether you're helping them use it well."

**Simple Signature**
> "I'm Bayram, founder of Onsa. If you want to talk about any of this, find me on LinkedIn."

**The Meta Reflection**
> "Reading through these transcripts painted a messier picture of AI adoption than the marketing materials suggest."

## Story-Driven Opening Examples

### Before (Generic AI Opening)
> "Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing the sales industry. Companies worldwide are adopting AI-powered tools to streamline their prospecting workflows and improve efficiency. In this article, we'll explore how AI agents differ from traditional software."

### After (Story-Driven Opening)
> "I was reviewing agent logs late one night when I found something I didn't program. One of our customers needed annual compensation data to qualify leads. The problem: most LinkedIn profiles don't show salary. Here's what our AI agent did instead."

### Why It Works
- Specific moment in time ("late one night")
- Concrete problem ("compensation data")
- Tension/curiosity ("something I didn't program")
- Promise of revelation ("Here's what happened")

## Weaving In Specifics

### Weak (Vague)
> "Many contacts in the database were outdated."

### Strong (Specific)
> "10-15% of contacts were essentially useless. Not slightly outdated—completely stale. Profiles that didn't exist on LinkedIn anymore. People who'd changed jobs 6+ months ago."

### Weak (Abstract Example)
> "One customer found our tool helpful for their niche use case."

### Strong (Specific Example)
> "One of our customers sells drone management software for construction and mining. Their ICP includes licensed drone operators—which are tracked in FAA registries. Our agents pull from these public registries as seed data, then expand to find companies and employees with titles like 'Chief Remote Pilot.'"

## Honest Tradeoff Patterns

### The Competitor Acknowledgment
> "I'm not going to pretend Apollo is bad. It's not. There are scenarios where it's the right choice: if you need volume fast, if you have sales ops resources, if your ICP is broad."

### The Self-Awareness
> "Here's the tension with agents: workarounds can be brilliant or dangerous. The Swiss salary lookup? Brilliant. But I've also seen agents find 'creative' interpretations of guidelines that technically comply but miss the intent."

### The Uncertainty
> "I've thought about this a lot since reading it. Is AI helping prevent burnout? Are we building coping mechanisms into our tools without realizing it? I don't have answers."

## Anti-Pattern Examples

### The Listicle Trap
**Bad:**
> "7 Ways AI Is Transforming Sales Prospecting
> 1. Automated research
> 2. Personalized outreach
> 3. ..."

**Good:**
> "I found something in these 36 interviews that made me rethink a feature we were about to ship."

### The Superlative Trap
**Bad:**
> "Our revolutionary AI technology delivers game-changing results with unprecedented accuracy."

**Good:**
> "Our approach is slower than pulling a list from a database. If you need 5,000 contacts by tomorrow, we're not the right choice."

### The CTA Trap
**Bad:**
> "Ready to transform your sales pipeline? Book a demo today and see how our AI-powered solution can help you close more deals!"

**Good:**
> "I'm Bayram, founder of Onsa. If you want to talk about any of this—or tell me where I'm wrong—find me on LinkedIn."

### The Hedge Trap
**Bad:**
> "This approach might potentially help some organizations possibly improve their outcomes in certain scenarios."

**Good:**
> "This worked for us. It might not work for everyone. Here's what we learned."

## Real Before/After: Apollo Article

### Before (Generic SEO)
> "Looking for an Apollo.io alternative that offers better AI-powered lead qualification and more efficient prospecting workflows? You're not alone. While Apollo has established itself as a go-to sales intelligence platform, many sales teams are discovering that modern AI SDR tools can dramatically improve their outbound efficiency."

### After (Founder Voice)
> "I should be upfront: I'm the founder of Onsa, a competing product. So yes, I'm biased. But I'm also someone who used Apollo extensively before deciding to build an alternative. This is that story.

If you're researching Apollo alternatives, you'll find dozens of listicles comparing features and pricing. This isn't that. This is what I actually experienced, what broke, and why I ended up building something with a completely different philosophy."

### What Changed
1. **Honesty upfront** - Acknowledges bias immediately
2. **Personal stake** - "I used it before building"
3. **Anti-listicle positioning** - "This isn't that"
4. **Promise of substance** - "What actually happened"

## Interview Question Bank

Use these to extract authentic content:

### Experience Extraction
- "Walk me through the first time you encountered this problem"
- "What was the exact moment you realized the old approach wasn't working?"
- "Tell me about a specific failure before you figured this out"

### Specifics Extraction
- "What percentage exactly?" (push for numbers)
- "Name a specific customer example"
- "What exact words did they use?"
- "How long did that actually take?"

### Honesty Extraction
- "What do your competitors genuinely do better?"
- "Where does your approach fall short?"
- "What's the main reason someone shouldn't use this?"
- "What surprised you most—in a bad way?"

### Story Extraction
- "Tell me about the customer that made you rethink everything"
- "What's the weirdest thing that's happened with your product?"
- "Describe a moment when you thought 'this is actually working'"

## Voice Matching Checklist

Before drafting, confirm you understand:

- [ ] Their typical opening style
- [ ] Average paragraph length (short punchy vs long flowing)
- [ ] First person usage (I vs we vs company name)
- [ ] Technical vocabulary level
- [ ] Use of humor (and what kind)
- [ ] How they cite data (inline vs footnotes vs none)
- [ ] Typical article length
- [ ] How they handle uncertainty/caveats
- [ ] Their closing signature style
